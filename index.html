        How to Play

        Mark calls

        Numbers are drawn one at a time and shown on the lantern ball. If a called number is on your card, select that cell to mark it (Enter or tap).

        Complete the pattern

        Each round names a target pattern — a line, corners, a frame, or the full card. The objective panel always shows the current target.

        Claim first

        When your marks complete the target pattern, press Claim (C) before the other lanterns do. False claims cost score.

        Keyboard

        ←→↑↓ move between cells, Enter marks (Space marks a focused cell, otherwise calls), C claim, U undo in Practice, H hint, P pause, R reset camera, Esc back.

        Scoring

        Pattern 1000 · each line +50 · each mark +10 · speed bonus under par · invalid action −25. Ties break on completion, fewer invalid actions, then faster claim.

        Fair play

        Every round is seeded and replayable. Daily seeds are fixed per UTC day and identical for everyone.
